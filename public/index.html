How we cook

Three things, done properly

Everything on the menu traces back to one of these — the oven, the stock pot, or the market box that arrives Tuesdays.

01

The oven

Built from reclaimed brick and fed oak all day. Bread at 7am, pizza at noon, whatever needs roasting after that.

02

The pot

A stock that hasn't stopped simmering since we opened. Every braise, soup, and risotto starts from the same base.

03

The box

Produce from three farms within an hour's drive. The menu changes when the box does, not the other way around.
